# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a place where careful engineers — across spaceflight, AI safety, web, and adjacent fields — can do real work together. That means:

- **Be technically honest.** Cite your sources. Mark estimates as estimates. Don't oversell readiness.
- **Be respectful in review.** Critique the work, not the person. Assume the other side knows something you don't.
- **Be inclusive.** This project welcomes contributors regardless of background, identity, or seniority. Junior contributors with a careful approach are as valuable as principal engineers.
- **Be safe.** Do not propose changes that quietly weaken failsafes. Safety is the architecture; it is not negotiable for convenience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption of discussion, doxxing, and any form of discrimination are not tolerated and will result in removal from the project.

## Enforcement

If you see behaviour that violates this code, report it privately to the maintainer at the address listed in `pyproject.toml`. Reports are confidential. The maintainer will respond within 7 days and act proportionately — from a private conversation to a permanent ban.

The full Contributor Covenant v2.1 text is the authoritative reference for ambiguous cases.
